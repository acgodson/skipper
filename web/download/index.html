What happens next

Open the file you downloaded and drag SkipperDesk to your Applications folder.

Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS asks you to confirm it was downloaded from the internet — click Open.

SkipperDesk asks for two permissions, one at a time, and shows you where to grant each. Then sign in, and the Mac is ready to share.

What you need

A Mac with Apple silicon — an M1 or later — running macOS 13 Ventura or newer. Intel Macs are not supported yet.

The Mac you connect from needs the app too, but it does not need an account and it does not need to be shared.

Your first five hours

Signing in adds five free hours to your account. They last 14 days, and they are only spent while a session is actually running.

After that you top up $5 at a time, which is 50 hours. There is no monthly bill and nothing to cancel.

Keeping it current

SkipperDesk updates itself. You do not need to come back here.
