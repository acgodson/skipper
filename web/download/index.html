A few steps.
Then you’re home.

  1. Put SkipperDesk in Applications

    Open the downloaded file and drag SkipperDesk into your Applications folder. Install it on both Macs.

  2. Open it on the Mac you’ll share

    The first time, macOS asks you to confirm the download. Choose Open. SkipperDesk then walks you through two permissions, one at a time.

  3. Sign in and turn on sharing

    On the Mac you connect from, enter the other Mac’s Device ID and access code. No account is needed on that Mac.

Two Macs.
One familiar desktop.

Both Macs need Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 Ventura or newer. Intel Macs aren’t supported yet.

Leaving the other Mac on its own? Turn on Unattended access before you go, so it won’t wait for someone to accept your connection.
