A familiar place to land

Different place.
Same desktop.

SkipperDesk puts one Mac’s screen on another. Your keyboard and trackpad control the other Mac, so you can pick up where you left off.

No moving your files. No setting everything up again.

  1. Leave a Mac ready

    Install SkipperDesk on both Macs. Sign in on the Mac you want to share, then turn on sharing.

  2. Make yourself at home

    On the Mac with you, enter the other Mac’s Device ID and access code. Its desktop opens in your window.

  3. You decide who gets in

    The other Mac asks before letting anyone in. Going out? Turn on Unattended access there before you leave.
