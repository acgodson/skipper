SkipperDesk

Use your Mac from anywhere.

SkipperDesk puts one Mac's screen on another. Work on the Mac you left at home from the Mac you have with you — the same desktop, the same files, the same apps.

Download for Mac

For Macs with Apple silicon, running macOS 13 Ventura or newer. Starts with five free hours.

Share this Mac

Turn on sharing and the Mac shows a Device ID and an access code. That is everything another Mac needs to reach it.

Connect to a Mac

Type the Device ID and the access code, and the other Mac's desktop fills your window. Your keyboard and trackpad go straight to it.

You decide every time

A Mac you are sharing asks you before it lets anyone in, and no answer declines. If you would rather it did not ask, you turn that on yourself.

What you pay

SkipperDesk starts with five free hours, spent only while a session is actually running. Paid top-ups are not open yet; when they are it will be $5 at a time, which is 50 hours, with no monthly bill and nothing to cancel.

One account, as many Macs as you like

Sign in once on each Mac you want to share. The Mac you are connecting from does not need an account at all.
