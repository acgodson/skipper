SkipperDesk is in beta. Your Mac shows the hours you have left — this page explains where those hours come from, why there is nothing to buy yet, and how to reach us.

Your hours

Signing in adds five hours of hosting. They last 14 days, and they are only spent while a session is actually running — a Mac that is shared but idle costs nothing.

The app shows how many hours are left, in its Account section. This page says the same thing to everyone, so go by the figure on your Mac.

There is nothing to buy yet

Paid top-ups are not open during the beta. When they open it will be $5 at a time, which is 50 hours, charged by the hour only while a session is running — no monthly bill and nothing to cancel.

Until then, email us if you need more hours and we will add them.

If you run out of hours

A Mac with no hours left stops accepting connections. Nothing is deleted and nothing is cancelled: the Mac keeps its Device ID and its access code, and it starts accepting connections again as soon as it has hours.

That Mac can still be the one you connect from. Only the Mac being shared needs hours.

Reach us

Email support@skipperdesk.app — about hours, about a Mac that will not connect, or about anything the app did that you did not expect.

One person reads that mailbox, so a reply takes a day or two rather than minutes.
