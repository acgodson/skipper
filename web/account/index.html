Your hours, plainly explained.

SkipperDesk is in beta. Your Mac shows the hours you have left; this page explains how they work and what is available today.

Hours during the beta

Signing in adds five hours of hosting. They last 14 days and are only spent while a session is running. A Mac that is shared but idle costs nothing.

The app shows your remaining balance in its Account section. Check your Mac for the current figure.

There is nothing to buy yet

Paid top-ups are not open during the beta. When they are available, $5 will add 50 hours. Hours will be charged only while a session is running, with no monthly bill and nothing to cancel.

For now, email us if you need more hours and we will add them.

If you run out

A Mac with no hours left stops accepting connections. Nothing is deleted or cancelled. It keeps its Device ID and access code, and starts accepting connections again as soon as it has hours.

That Mac can still be the one you connect from. Only the Mac being shared needs hours.

Reach support

Email support@skipperdesk.app about hours, a Mac that will not connect, or anything the app did that you did not expect.

One person reads that mailbox, so a reply usually takes one or two days.
